## Prompt

Create one complete DisplayLab focus island as a premium bright cozy 2.5D orthographic diorama. Use the same fixed 35°/45° camera, upper-left sunlight, turquoise surrounding water, matte pale stone, warm wood, teal cloth, glass/prism accents, lush but disciplined foliage, and lower-right soft shadows as the approved reference.

The island is a terraced crescent built around a clearly open presentation cove. Include a curved projection/display sail, a circular color-swatch garden, a prism/light-study landmark, a small observatory or optics workshop, and two separate empty level work stations planted on terrain for later Code Engineer and QA Navigator overlays. Keep both anchor floors unobstructed and separated from labels/landmarks.

## Hard constraints

- Exactly one complete island; no other islands, boat, people, humanoids, robots, or animals.
- No embedded text, labels, letters, numbers, signs, logos, watermarks, semantic UI, app chrome, control labels, or software interface hierarchy. Non-semantic abstract color-calibration boards, optical marks, and the projection sail are required physical DisplayLab landmarks and are allowed when they remain purely visual instrumentation.
- Do not alter the camera, light direction, material language, or horizon relative to the overview.
- Do not block the presentation cove, swatch system, prism, or two empty work anchors.

## Landmark checklist

- Open presentation cove with curved display/projection structure.
- Color swatch garden and prism/light-study installation.
