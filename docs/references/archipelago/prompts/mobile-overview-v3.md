## Prompt

Create a tall mobile overview of the same SidequestLab Living Archipelago as the desktop production prompt. Preserve the exact visual lineage: bright premium cozy 2.5D miniature, fixed three-quarter orthographic camera, soft upper-left sunlight, lower-right contact shadows, turquoise water, matte stone/plaster, warm wood, glass, cloth, ceramic, and restrained greenery.

Arrange exactly three complete, readable project islands in a vertical journey with generous water separation: DisplayLab first with its terraced presentation cove and swatch/prism system; BookSalon second with its rounded reading courtyard, curved library terraces, greenhouse, and communal table; N-Bang third with its split home clusters, one equal-share bridge, ledger/counter kiosk, and productive shared garden. One small Navigator boat may appear between islands. Preserve crop-safe space above and below and do not cut any project landmark.

## Hard constraints

- Same three project identities as desktop; do not reskin or reorder their internal landmarks.
- Exactly three project islands, zero people/Wayfarers/robots, and at most one small Navigator boat.
- No embedded text, letters, numbers, labels, signs, logos, watermark, semantic UI, app chrome, cards, browser/phone frame, captions, or buttons. DisplayLab's non-semantic abstract color-calibration and projection surfaces remain required physical landmarks; color grids or optical marks are allowed only when they do not form readable language, control labels, or a software interface hierarchy.
- No scaled desktop screenshot; author a legible portrait composition with fixed camera/light/materials.
- Avoid generic placeholder islands, extra islets, infantile toy treatment, pirate/military themes, dark cyberpunk, or photorealism.

## Acceptance landmarks

- DisplayLab: open presentation cove + swatch/prism system.
- BookSalon: reading courtyard + greenhouse/book terraces.
- N-Bang: split-neighborhood bridge + ledger/shared garden.
