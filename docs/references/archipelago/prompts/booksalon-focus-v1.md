## Prompt

Create one complete BookSalon focus island as a premium bright cozy 2.5D orthographic diorama. Preserve the reference camera and upper-left light, turquoise water, matte stone, cream plaster, forest green tile, warm wood, glass greenhouse, ceramic planters, and restrained flowering foliage.

The island is a rounded garden ring around a quiet central reading courtyard. Include a welcoming reading house, curved open book terraces, a lantern path, a greenhouse reading nook, and a large communal round table. Provide two separate empty level desk/terrain anchors for later Code Engineer and QA Navigator overlays without placing figures.

## Hard constraints

- Exactly one complete island; no other islands, boat, people, humanoids, robots, or animals.
- No text, readable book titles, labels, letters, numbers, pseudo-writing, signs, logos, watermarks, or UI.
- Keep fixed orthographic camera, upper-left light, material scale, and clear shoreline.
- Avoid infantile nursery motifs, giant props, merchandise mascots, or generic greenhouse-only identity.

## Landmark checklist

- Reading house with curved book terraces around a quiet courtyard.
- Greenhouse/lantern garden plus communal round table.
